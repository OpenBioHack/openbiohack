
## Stage-A faithfulness (driver pass over this output)
Every load-bearing figure — a dose, a mechanism claim, a sensitivity — traces to a cited source, OR is
marked "gaps for upstream / no evidence found". An uncited, unflagged figure FAILS (this is what stops
a fabricated figure reaching the user behind green gates).
